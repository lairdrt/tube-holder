## Short description

A compact 10-slot organizer for upright medicine and ointment tubes, with graduated compartment widths and two slide-in magnets that hold it against a steel-backed medicine cabinet. **Physically tested and working on the first print.**

## Description

Ointment and medicine tubes tend to fall over in a medicine cabinet. This single-piece organizer keeps ten tubes standing upright and easy to retrieve.

The holder has two rows of five compartments. Each row uses five clear widths — **35 / 30 / 25 / 25 / 25 mm** — with vertical openings in the walls so tubes remain easy to access. A 10 mm base supports the tubes, while the organizer's overall height is only 65 mm.

Two magnet holders project about **5 mm** beyond the rear wall so the magnets can contact the cabinet's steel wall above its curved lower corner. The magnets slide in from the top and are held in place by broad retaining clips.

**The approved V4 design passed its first physical print:** all tubes fit, the organizer fits the cabinet, and the magnets securely hold it in place.

### Exact magnets used — verified fit

**2 × Radial Magnets SKU 9404, N35 neodymium disks, Ø20 × 2 mm.** These magnets were physically tested and fit the V4 clips perfectly.

https://radialmagnet.com/our-magnets/neodymium-magnet-disk-n35-20mm-x-2mma

### Key dimensions

- Body: **155 W × 85 D × 65 H mm**; **90 mm** overall depth including magnet pockets
- Compartments: **2 × 5**, width sequence **35 / 30 / 25 / 25 / 25 mm** per row
- Each compartment is **39.5 mm deep**
- Base: **10 mm**; walls rise **55 mm** above the base

Standard Bambu Studio settings were used **without manual changes**.

### Assembly

1. Print the verified STL with the base flat.
2. Slide one **20 × 2 mm** magnet into each of the two holders from the top.
3. Place the organizer against the steel cabinet back.
4. Insert tubes into suitable compartments.
